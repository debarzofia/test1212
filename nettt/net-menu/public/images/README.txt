PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================

Copy your photos into this folder (public/images). They are served at /images/<file name>.

Files the menu expects (names are case-sensitive):

  logo.jpg                   (NET logo - used everywhere as the logo)
  hummus.jpg
  kibbeh.jpg
  lentil-soup.jpg
  Image 2.jpg                (tabbouleh)
  12.jpg                     (mixed grill)
  13.jpg                     (lamb kebab)
  shish-tawook.jpg
  dolma.jpg
  chicken-biryani.jpg
  qozi.jpg
  masgouf.jpg
  baklava.jpg
  kunafa.jpg
  kleicha.jpg
  tea.jpg
  arabic-coffee.jpg
  doogh.jpg
  pomegranate-juice.jpg

To change a file name, edit the `img` field in data/menu.js.
A dish whose photo file is missing shows the NET logo instead.
